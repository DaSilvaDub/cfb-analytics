# Saturday Execution Cheat Sheet
**Source of Truth:** Post-settlement calibration across 2026-09-19 & 2026-09-26 slates.  
**Rule:** Open every Saturday slate directly from this sheet. No exceptions.

---

## 1. Locked Rules (Non-Negotiable)

1. **Tomato Box Scores Out**
   - Strip cupcake blowouts and non-FBS stats entirely. Project offensive floors and defensive resistance strictly from FBS tape.
2. **Game Passing > Team Passing**
   - Passing volume carries across game scripts even during trailing blowouts, garbage time, or backup QB relief. Never take team passing over game passing.
3. **Dead-Offense Trench Unders**
   - When both offenses are physically dead against elite defensive fronts (the Iowa–Michigan / Texas–Tennessee profile), **play the Under every single time**.
   - **Do not add a favorite side** to those games (Michigan -5.5 lost; Iowa +5.5 won).
4. **3-0 Run-Smash Favorite Invariant**
   - A 3-0 favorite averaging 200+ rush yards playing its first road game is **smash first, weather under second**.
   - Project the favorite's scoring floor first. Never build an under that requires a 3-0 smash favorite to score $\le 17$.
5. **Thin Dog Trap Veto**
   - Thin home dogs (+2.5 to +6.0) after a road 2H shutout = **KILL / VETO**.
   - Thin dogs (+8.5 vs a 3-0 road smash favorite) are traps, not fat dogs (+20 late kick).
6. **Team Rush at the Honest Midpoint = PASS**
   - If a team rushing line sits at the honest statistical midpoint with no decisive scheme or trench edge, pass immediately (e.g. Florida 201). Do not force action on fair numbers.
7. **Confirmed QB1 Out = No Live Add**
   - If a starting QB exits the game, all live adds are immediately banned. Existing pre-game tickets stand.
8. **Halftime Raises Are Banned**
   - Never raise or bump a total at halftime based on first-half scoring alone unless both starting QB1s are confirmed healthy and Q3 is proven on pace.
9. **Stand, Don't Add**
   - When a home power favorite covers cleanly (e.g. Alabama -12.5), stand. Do not press, chase, or add live risk.
10. **Night Over Needs Q4 Pace**
    - A night over ticket requires 4th quarter volume expectation, not just a brisk Q2.
11. **Half-Point Hook Grading**
    - Half-point totals (e.g. 47.5, 57.5) across key scoring thresholds carry elevated hook variance. Grade at 6.5 confidence maximum; never 7.1+.
12. **One Scoreboard Pass**
    - Confirm West Coast / late kickoffs are officially live or final with a single clean scoreboard pass before discussing or grading tickets.
