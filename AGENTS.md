# cfb-analytics Repository Guidelines & Invariants

## Operational Invariants
1. **Shadow Mode**: Nothing in this repository is decision-grade until Phase 4 clears the two-key promotion gate in `config/promotion.json`. Always stamp generated outputs with `UNPROMOTED - shadow output, not decision-grade`.
2. **US Eastern Slate Dates**: A slate is a US Eastern calendar date, never a UTC date. Kickoffs must be converted to `FOOTBALL_TZ` (`America/New_York`) when determining slate membership.
3. **Outlier Session**: Outlier tokens live 24 hours. The pipeline degrades gracefully if expired; refresh the session via the `outlier` project when needed.
4. **Devig & Consensus**: Devig each book against itself first, then aggregate. Spreads group on absolute line values. Negative hold and placeholder quotes (-100000) must be dropped/flagged.
5. **Canonical Game Identity**: CFBD keys (`cfbd:<id>`) are canonical; Outlier resolves onto CFBD games and writes no games or teams of its own. Games link to venues by `venue_id`, never by venue name.
6. **Ranked OVER board**: `over-board` is AP Top 25 games plus each conference's top two. Markets are team rushing, game rushing, team receiving, game receiving — OVER only, ranked by calibrated model probability (capped at 84%), with US Eastern kickoff times. Favorite receiving is dropped at spread ≤ −20 (sit-QB). Combined game rushing is dropped at spread ≥ 14 (blowout script kills combined volume). SP+/SRS must not substitute for the AP poll. Player props are prohibited. Publishing freezes `over_board_snapshots`. `settle` grades that snapshot against `team_game_box` (rushing_yards / net_passing_yards), never a live rebuild. Close games do not auto-inflate pass volume. Calibrate by family (`favorite_team_rush` / `team_rec` / `game_yards`); size up a family only after ≥70% over 3–4 Saturdays. Game passing > team passing (passing volume carries even in adversity). Rushing overs qualify only when lines are deflated (<100 yards) or opponent rush defense allows ≥190 rush yards/game. Team rush at the honest midpoint = pass.
7. **Multi-Factor Reasoning & Negative Gate**: Never recommend a favorite solely because of market price, ranking, or seed. Every recommended Moneyline, Spread, Total, or Team Prop must pass through the multi-factor reasoning engine evaluating six core dimensions: (1) Honest vs. Junk tape (cupcake blowouts stripped, FBS EPA floor/ceiling), (2) Starting QB continuity & backup status, (3) Weather multipliers & away travel fatigue tax, (4) Trench health & OL/DL attrition differential, (5) Program structure, coaching stability & rest disparity, and (6) Mathematical edge vs. devigged consensus. Any favorite where situational data contradicts the market line must be flagged or downgraded with an explicit counter-thesis.
8. **Grok Decision Rules Governance**: All market picks and mispriced candidates must adhere to the decision rules in `docs/grok_rules.md` (Rules A–F: Home Power Smash, Montana Rule QB verification, Fat Road Dogs, Thin Dog Trap rejection, High-Scoring Conference Over, 1H Preferred). Thin underdogs (+1.0 to +3.5) without opposing QB injury or dominant trench mismatch are strictly vetoed under Rule D. Thin home dogs +2.5 to +6 after a road 2H shutout = kill.
9. **Team Props Slate Input Mapping**: `load_team_props_inputs_for_slate` returns a dictionary indexed by `team_id` (`dict[str, TeamPropsInputs]`), never `game_id`. Feature extractors and scanners iterating over slate games must resolve inputs using `home_team_id` and `away_team_id` from the `games` table.
10. **Underdog Rushing & Script Decoupling**: Underdogs facing spreads ≥ +14 in projected trailing scripts discard rushing volume in favor of passing. Programmatically disqualify underdog team rushing OVER candidates from the ranked board on spreads ≥ +14 unless the team possesses a dominant trench rushing identity and an opponent allowing ≥ 190 rush yards/game. Do not assign ≥ 75% confidence or HIGH tier to underdog team rushing props.
11. **Service Academy Road Favorite Caution**: One-dimensional option/service academy road favorites carrying heavy market prices (≤ -200) face elevated variance against rested home athletic fronts. Never treat service academy road chalk as anchor parlay legs without affirmative passing threat or decisive trench EPA superiority.
12. **Card Execution & Halftime Total Lock**: Halftime total raises/adds are strictly banned unless both QB1s are confirmed in the game AND Q3 is proven on pace. When a home favorite covers cleanly (e.g. Alabama -12.5), stand, don't press or add live risk. Confirmed QB1 out = no live add. Night overs need Q4 pace, not just Q2 pace.
13. **3-0 Run-Smash vs. Weather Under Invariant**: Never dress a 3-0 run-smash favorite averaging 200+ rush yards (e.g. Utah) as a weather under. First road game for a 3-0 smash team is smash first, weather under second. Project the favorite's scoring floor first; never build an under that requires a 3-0 smash team to score ≤ 17. Thin dogs (+8.5 vs road smash) are traps and not the same ticket as fat dogs (+20 late kick).
14. **Dead-Offense Trench Unders**: Play the Iowa–Michigan / Texas–Tennessee profile (both offenses dead against elite fronts) every time it appears. Play the under; do not add a favorite side (e.g. Michigan -5.5 would have lost; Iowa +5.5 won).
15. **Half-Point Hook Grading**: Half-point totals (e.g. 47.5, 57.5) landing on the other side of key numbers carry elevated hook risk. Grade those 6.5 confidence max, not 7.1+.
